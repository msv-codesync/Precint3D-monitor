Precint3D

Watch the print.
Pause if it fails.

Point a camera at the part. The app watches for spaghetti, a bad first layer, a shift, and other common fails. If you connect OctoPrint, it can pause the printer for you.

Open the app
  1. Aim the camera at the plastic, not the empty bed.
  2. Press Start watching.
  3. Optional: add OctoPrint and tick Pause the printer if a fail holds.
The public page is this site. Detection runs on your computer, with your camera.
